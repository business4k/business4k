Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 26, 2026

Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Business 4K ("we", "us", or "our") handles information when you visit our website. We are committed to being transparent about our practices, even though we currently collect minimal information.

Information We Collect

Business 4K does not currently require visitors to create an account or provide personal information to access the website.

Like most websites, basic technical information may be automatically processed by our hosting provider when you visit the site. This may include your IP address, browser type, device type, referring pages, and pages requested. This information is used solely for the operation, security, and maintenance of the website.

Cookies and Tracking

Business 4K does not currently use its own cookies for advertising, analytics, or user tracking.

Third-party services may introduce cookies or similar technologies if we add features such as analytics, advertising, or embedded content in the future. If we do so, we will update this policy to describe those services and, where required by law, request your consent.

Data Retention

Technical information processed by our hosting provider is retained only for as long as necessary for security, troubleshooting, and operational purposes, in accordance with the hosting provider's own retention practices.

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights regarding any personal information collected about you, including the right to request access, correction, or deletion of that information.

Since Business 4K does not currently collect personal information directly from visitors, there is generally no personal data for us to provide, correct, or delete. If this changes, this policy will be updated to explain how you can exercise these rights.

Children's Privacy

Business 4K is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or the relevant age of digital consent in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Third-Party Links

Business 4K may contain links to third-party websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices, content, or policies of those websites. We recommend reviewing their privacy policies before providing any personal information.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when new features, analytics services, advertising services, or other technologies are added to Business 4K. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect the most recent revision. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through the contact information provided on the Business 4K website.
